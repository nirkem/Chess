Welcome to Nir's Chess game!

In this game, we perform moves by specifying the origin square and the destination square.
For example, to move Pawn d2 to square d4, you need to enter: d2d4 in the console.

In case of a pawn promotion, enter the move followed by the symbol of the new piece.
For example, to move Pawn b7 to b8 and promote to queen, you need to enter: b7b8Q in the console

To castle, move the king two squares toward the rook, for example: e1g1 or e1c1.
En passant is played like any other pawn capture, for example: e5d6.

On the board, white pieces are represented by capital letters and black by lowercase letters.
Remember that white always starts the game.
